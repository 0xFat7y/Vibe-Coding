NUTRITION GUIDE

Fuel your body for optimal calisthenics performance. Learn what to eat, when, and why.

NUTRITION FUNDAMENTALS

Build your body from the inside out

Protein

1.6-2.2g per kg bodyweight
Essential for muscle recovery and growth. Aim for lean sources spread throughout the day.

Carbohydrates

3-5g per kg bodyweight
Your primary energy source. Focus on complex carbs for sustained energy during training.

Healthy Fats

0.8-1.2g per kg bodyweight
Support hormone production and joint health. Include omega-3s for recovery.

DIET APPROACHES

Balanced Macro Diet

The most versatile approach for calisthenics athletes. Balance protein, carbs, and fats to support training while maintaining a healthy body composition.

  • ✓ Flexible and sustainable
  • ✓ Good for beginners
  • ✓ Supports all training goals

Plant-Based Athlete

Fully plant-based nutrition for calisthenics. Focus on complete protein sources, adequate calories, and proper supplementation (B12, D3).

  • ✓ High in micronutrients
  • ✓ Good for recovery
  • ✓ Requires planning

Intermittent Fasting

Time-restricted eating (16:8 or 18:6). Can work well for calisthenics if training is properly timed and sufficient calories are consumed.

  • ✓ May improve insulin sensitivity
  • ✓ Convenient for some
  • ✓ Not for everyone

High Protein Focus

Elevated protein intake (2-2.5g/kg) to maximize muscle recovery and support strength gains. Ideal during intensive training phases.

  • ✓ Great for muscle building
  • ✓ Supports recovery
  • ✓ Very satiating

FOODS TO LIMIT OR AVOID

❌ Highly Processed Foods

  • • Fast food and deep-fried items
  • • Sugary cereals and pastries
  • • Processed meats (hot dogs, deli meats)
  • • Chips and packaged snacks

🚫 Sugar & Empty Calories

  • • Soda and energy drinks
  • • Candy and sweets
  • • White bread and refined grains
  • • Excessive alcohol

MEAL PREP TIPS

Prepare your meals in advance to stay consistent with your nutrition goals

✓ Cook in batches on weekends
✓ Use portion control containers
✓ Prep protein sources ahead
✓ Keep healthy snacks ready
✓ Stay hydrated (3-4L daily)
✓ Track your intake initially
