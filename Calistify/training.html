TRAINING PROGRAMS

Your complete roadmap from absolute beginner to elite calisthenics athlete. Follow our structured progression system and unlock your body's full potential.

PRE-BEGINNER

Foundation Level

Build Your Foundation

Start your calisthenics journey from absolute zero. This level focuses on developing the basic strength, mobility, and body awareness needed for safe and effective training. Perfect for complete beginners or those returning after a long break.

40+Exercises
8-12Weeks
BasicDifficulty
  • Basic bodyweight movements
  • Mobility and flexibility work
  • Proper form development
  • Core strength building
  • Wrist and joint conditioning
  • Movement pattern learning
02

BEGINNER

Fundamental Skills

Master the Fundamentals

Build solid strength with the fundamental calisthenics exercises. Master proper push-ups, pull-ups, dips, squats, and core work. Develop the strength base needed for intermediate progressions.

80+Exercises
12-16Weeks
ModerateDifficulty
  • Full push-up mastery
  • Proper pull-up technique
  • Parallel bar dips
  • Advanced core exercises
  • Bodyweight squats & lunges
  • Introduction to static holds
03

INTERMEDIATE

Advanced Skills

Unlock Advanced Movements

Progress to impressive skills that demonstrate real mastery. Learn muscle-ups, L-sits, handstand push-ups, pistol squats, and begin working on static strength holds like front lever and back lever progressions.

120+Exercises
16-24Weeks
AdvancedDifficulty
  • Muscle-up technique
  • Front lever progressions
  • Back lever holds
  • Handstand push-ups
  • Pistol squats
  • Advanced core work
04

EXPERT

Elite Mastery

Achieve Elite-Level Strength

Master the most demanding calisthenics skills. Work towards full planche, one-arm pull-ups, iron cross, 90-degree push-ups, and other movements that represent the pinnacle of bodyweight strength.

150+Exercises
24+Weeks
EliteDifficulty
  • Full planche progressions
  • One-arm pull-up training
  • Iron cross work
  • 90-degree push-ups
  • Human flag progressions
  • Advanced freestyle combinations
