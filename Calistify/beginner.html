Level Overview

The Beginner level builds upon your pre-beginner foundation. You'll master the fundamental calisthenics movements: perfect push-ups, pull-ups, dips, and advanced core work. This level transforms you into a capable athlete ready for intermediate progressions.

Pull Mastery

Achieve multiple clean pull-ups and chin-ups. Build strong back muscles and develop the pulling strength essential for advanced skills.

Push Power

Perfect your push-ups and progress to dips. Develop chest, shoulders, and triceps strength for advanced pressing movements.

Core Foundation

Advanced planks, leg raises, and core compression. Build the abdominal strength needed for levers and advanced skills.

Level Completion Requirements

To complete Beginner level and progress to Intermediate, demonstrate mastery of these movements:

Minimum Standards

  • ✓ 10 consecutive pull-ups
  • ✓ 12 consecutive dips
  • ✓ 30 consecutive push-ups
  • ✓ 15 diamond push-ups
  • ✓ 15 hanging leg raises
  • ✓ 20-second L-sit hold
  • ✓ 5 pistol squats each leg
  • ✓ 60-second plank hold

Bonus Goals

  • ★ 15+ pull-ups
  • ★ 20+ dips
  • ★ First muscle-up attempt
  • ★ 30-second L-sit
  • ★ 10 pistol squats each leg
  • ★ Handstand hold (wall)
  • ★ Tuck front lever
  • ★ Archer push-ups

12-Week Training Program

Train 4 times per week. Focus on progressive overload and perfect form.

Weeks 1-4: Foundation

Day 1: Push

  • • Push-Ups: 4x15-20
  • • Dips (assisted): 4x8-12
  • • Diamond Push-Ups: 3x10-15
  • • Plank: 3x60s

Day 2: Pull

  • • Pull-Ups: 5x5-8
  • • Chin-Ups: 4x6-10
  • • Australian Rows: 3x15
  • • Dead Hang: 3x30-45s

Weeks 5-8: Strength Building

Day 1: Push

  • • Dips: 5x10-15
  • • Push-Ups: 4x20-30
  • • Pike Push-Ups: 3x10-15
  • • L-Sit: 4x15-20s

Day 2: Pull

  • • Pull-Ups: 5x8-12
  • • Negative Pull-Ups: 3x5
  • • Hanging Leg Raises: 4x12-15
  • • Scapular Pulls: 3x15

Weeks 9-12: Advanced Progressions

Focus Areas

  • • Weighted pull-ups & dips
  • • Pistol squat progressions
  • • L-sit holds (30+ seconds)
  • • Introduction to muscle-up
  • • Handstand practice
  • • Tuck front lever attempts

Week 12: Testing

Final week is for testing all requirements. Record your numbers and celebrate your progress!

If you meet the minimum standards, you're ready for Intermediate Level. If not, repeat weeks 9-12 until requirements are met.
