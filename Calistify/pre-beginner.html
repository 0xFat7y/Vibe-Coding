Level Overview

The Pre-Beginner level is designed for complete beginners or those returning to fitness after a long break. This level focuses on building the fundamental strength, mobility, and movement patterns required for safe and effective calisthenics training.

What You'll Learn

  • Proper push-up form (from knees to full)
  • Basic bodyweight squats
  • Plank holds and core engagement
  • Assisted pull-up progressions
  • Basic mobility and flexibility
  • Wrist and joint conditioning
  • Body awareness and control
  • Proper breathing techniques

Training Focus Areas

Foundation Strength

Build basic pushing, pulling, and core strength through modified exercises that prepare your body for more advanced movements.

Mobility Work

Develop the flexibility and joint mobility necessary for safe training and injury prevention throughout your journey.

Mind-Muscle Connection

Learn to feel and control each muscle group, developing the neurological pathways for efficient movement.

Level Requirements

To successfully complete the Pre-Beginner level and progress to Beginner, you should be able to perform these exercises with good form:

Minimum Standards to Progress

  • 20 consecutive wall push-ups
  • 15 consecutive knee push-ups
  • 10 consecutive incline push-ups
  • 30-second plank hold
  • 20 bodyweight squats
  • 10 Australian pull-ups
  • 15-second dead hang
  • 10 forward lunges each leg

Take Your Time

There's no rush to progress. Focus on building solid form and strength. Most people complete this level in 8-12 weeks, but your timeline may vary. Quality over speed!

8-Week Training Program

Follow this structured program 3-4 times per week. Allow at least one rest day between sessions.

Weeks 1-2: Foundation

Workout A (Mon/Wed)

  • • Wall Push-Ups: 3 sets x 10-15 reps
  • • Dead Hang: 3 sets x 10-20 seconds
  • • Bodyweight Squats: 3 sets x 15 reps
  • • Plank: 3 sets x 20-30 seconds

Workout B (Fri/Sat)

  • • Incline Push-Ups: 3 sets x 8-12 reps
  • • Australian Pull-Ups: 3 sets x 5-8 reps
  • • Forward Lunges: 3 sets x 8/leg
  • • Side Plank: 3 sets x 15s/side

Weeks 3-4: Building Strength

Workout A

  • • Incline Push-Ups: 4 sets x 10-15 reps
  • • Dead Hang: 4 sets x 15-25 seconds
  • • Bodyweight Squats: 4 sets x 20 reps
  • • Plank: 4 sets x 30-45 seconds

Workout B

  • • Knee Push-Ups: 4 sets x 6-10 reps
  • • Australian Pull-Ups: 4 sets x 8-12 reps
  • • Bulgarian Split Squat: 3 sets x 8/leg
  • • Mountain Climbers: 3 sets x 20 total

Weeks 5-6: Progression

Workout A

  • • Knee Push-Ups: 4 sets x 8-12 reps
  • • Assisted Pull-Ups: 4 sets x 5-8 reps
  • • Jump Squats: 3 sets x 10 reps
  • • Plank to Down Dog: 3 sets x 10 reps

Workout B

  • • Eccentric Push-Ups: 4 sets x 5-8 reps
  • • Australian Pull-Ups: 4 sets x 10-15 reps
  • • Step-Ups: 3 sets x 12/leg
  • • Leg Raises: 3 sets x 8-12 reps

Weeks 7-8: Testing & Refinement

Workout A

  • • Eccentric Push-Ups: 4 sets x 6-10 reps
  • • Assisted Pull-Ups: 4 sets x 6-10 reps
  • • Squat Jumps: 4 sets x 12 reps
  • • Plank: 4 sets x 45-60 seconds

Week 8: Testing Week

Test all requirements to see if you're ready to progress to Beginner level. Record your numbers and celebrate your progress!
