Advanced Training

The Intermediate level introduces skills that most people find impressive. You'll learn muscle-ups, front lever progressions, handstand push-ups, and develop serious static strength.

Muscle-Up Mastery

The holy grail of calisthenics. Learn the explosive pull and transition that combines pull-ups and dips into one fluid movement.

Front Lever

Develop incredible back and core strength with front lever progressions. Build toward this iconic static hold.

Handstand Training

Progress from wall handstands to freestanding balance. Develop shoulder strength and body awareness.

Level Mastery Goals

Completion Standards

  • ✓ 5 clean muscle-ups
  • ✓ 10-second advanced tuck front lever
  • ✓ 15-second full back lever
  • ✓ 8 handstand push-ups
  • ✓ 10 archer pull-ups each side
  • ✓ 60-second handstand hold
  • ✓ 10 pistol squats each leg
  • ✓ 30-second L-sit hold

Advanced Progressions

Work toward these skills during Intermediate training. They'll prepare you for Expert level:

  • → Straddle front lever attempts
  • → One-arm pull-up negatives
  • → Tuck planche holds
  • → Freestanding handstand balance
  • → Human flag progressions
  • → Weighted muscle-ups
